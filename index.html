Why a bar?

Many people think science is just a pile of cold, complicated equations. To us, the way the universe works is incredibly beautiful — and best discussed somewhere relaxed, like your favourite local pub.

The name is a nod to ħ (“h-bar”), the reduced Planck constant at the heart of quantum mechanics. Every episode is a house special: one big idea, from magic-angle graphene to neutrinos at the South Pole, served in English and in Mandarin.
